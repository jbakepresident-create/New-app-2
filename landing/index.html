Questions

Is Steadily medical advice?

No. Steadily is a personal tracking tool. It records what your prescriber has told you to take. It never calculates, changes or recommends doses. Always follow your care team's guidance.

Which medications does it work with?

Any prescribed GLP-1 or GLP-1/GIP medication, whether it's a weekly injection or a daily pill. You enter your medication and schedule exactly as prescribed.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. At launch you'll be able to import weight and activity from Apple Health, and import a past log from a spreadsheet or another tracker's CSV export. You can export everything from Steadily the same way.

Where is my data stored? Do you sell it?

Your health log lives on your iPhone. An optional encrypted backup keeps it safe when you switch phones, and you can export everything anytime. We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

What happens when I stop my medication?

You keep tracking. Maintenance mode is built for life after the shot: keep logging weight, protein and activity, and see your trend against a range you set with your doctor.
